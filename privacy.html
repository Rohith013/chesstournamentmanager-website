Overview & Privacy Commitment

This Privacy Policy explains how Game Wings Support ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Chess Tournament Manager Android application (package ID com.chesstournamentmanager.app) and its companion official website.

Chess Tournament Manager is built around local device ownership of tournament records, paired with transparent disclosures regarding third-party advertising services used to provide the application free of charge.

1. Information Stored Locally & No User Accounts

  • No Account or Login Required: You do not register, create a profile, sign in, or submit an email address or password to use Chess Tournament Manager. Game Wings Support does not operate any account database or authentication server for this application.
  • On-Device SQLite Database: All data you enter—including tournament names, venues, start/end dates, player names, ratings, optional FIDE IDs, federations, titles, team rosters, per-round lineups, pairings, results, tie-break preferences, and activity undo logs—is stored locally on your device inside a private SQLite database alongside local app preferences (SharedPreferences).
  • No Automatic Cloud Synchronization: Version 1.1.3 does not upload, mirror, or synchronize your tournaments or player registries to servers controlled by Game Wings Support.
  • User-Initiated Exports & Backups: When you export a PDF, CSV, Excel (.xlsx), or .ctmbackup JSON file, the file is generated locally on your device. It is only transmitted externally if and when you actively share or save it through your device's Android Share sheet or file picker.
  • Email Support Correspondence: If you email gamewingssupport@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the message contents you choose to send solely to respond to your request.

2. First-Party Analytics & Diagnostic Logs

Game Wings Support does not integrate third-party behavioral analytics tools (such as Google Firebase Analytics) or third-party developer crash reporting SDKs (such as Crashlytics or Sentry) inside the application, and we do not operate a custom telemetry server.

Note that third-party advertising and platform services—specifically Google AdMob (described below) and the Google Play Store / Android operating system diagnostics managed by Google under your device settings—collect standard technical, diagnostic, and interaction data under Google's own privacy policies.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Service Providers (Google AdMob)

To offer Chess Tournament Manager free of charge, Version 1.1.3 uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to load and display advertisements on Android:

  • Ad Formats Used: In-feed Native ad cards (displayed on player, tournament, standings, settings, and export screens), Interstitial ads (when creating a tournament or finishing a tournament), and Rewarded ads (shown when exporting PDF, CSV, or Excel tournament reports when an ad is available; creating a .ctmbackup backup file does not trigger a rewarded ad).
  • Information Collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK: Depending on your region and consent settings, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (including the Android Advertising ID), IP address (which may indicate general geographic location), app set ID, device/OS version, ad impression/click events, and SDK crash or performance diagnostics.
  • Contextual Metadata: Ad requests generated by the app include chess-category contextual keywords and a contextual URL hint (https://www.chess.com) solely to request contextually relevant advertisements from AdMob; the app does not browse external websites on your behalf.

For full details on how Google processes advertising data, please review Google's policies and controls:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services
  • Google My Ad Center / Ad Personalization Settings

4. Consent Choices (EEA/UK GDPR & US State Privacy / CCPA)

  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): When the app starts in regions where consent is required (including the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), Google's UMP SDK checks consent status and presents a consent form before personalized ads are served.
  • Managing or Withdrawing Ad Consent: Version 1.1.3 does not yet include an in-app "Manage Ad Privacy" button inside the app's Settings screen. You can review, limit, or withdraw advertising personalization at any time by:
    • Adjusting your Android device settings (typically Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads to delete or reset your Advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization);
    • Managing ad preferences via Google My Ad Center; or
    • Clearing the application's storage via Android App Info (remember to export .ctmbackup files first, as clearing app storage deletes local tournaments) to reset stored UMP consent on your device.
  • California & US State Residents: Game Wings Support does not sell your personal tournament data. Third-party ad SDK identifier usage may constitute "sharing" or targeted advertising under applicable state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA; you can restrict ad personalization using Android's system ad controls described above.

5. App Permissions & Device Access

Permission / Access Purpose in Chess Tournament Manager
INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Used solely to check connectivity, fetch Google UMP consent configuration, and request Google AdMob advertisements. Core pairing, standings, backup, and export engines operate offline.
Scoped File Picker & Android Share Sheet Used only when you explicitly choose to import/restore a .ctmbackup file or share an exported PDF, CSV, Excel, or backup file through temporary scoped cache directories.
Camera, Location, Contacts, Microphone Not requested or accessed.

6. Data Retention, Security & Account / Data Deletion

Because Chess Tournament Manager has no user accounts and no cloud storage, Game Wings Support cannot access, view, recover, or remotely erase your local SQLite tournament database. You remain in complete direct control of local data deletion:

  • Delete Specific Tournaments or Players: Open any tournament or open the Players registry inside the app and use the Delete action to remove individual records from the local SQLite database immediately.
  • Reset App Preferences: Open Settings → Reset Settings in the app to restore default theme, format, and export preferences.
  • Remove All App Data Locally: Uninstalling Chess Tournament Manager—or using Android Settings → Apps → Chess Tournament Manager → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data—permanently deletes the local SQLite database, undo logs, and stored UMP preferences from your device. Exported PDFs, spreadsheets, or .ctmbackup files you previously saved to Downloads or shared externally must be deleted manually by you.
  • Reset Advertising Identifiers: Reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time under Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Delete Support Emails: If you emailed us at gamewingssupport@gmail.com, email correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve your inquiry, and you may email us at any time to request deletion of prior support emails.
Data Safety Reminder: Because data is stored exclusively on-device, uninstalling the app or clearing storage without saving a .ctmbackup file permanently destroys your tournaments and rosters.

7. Children's Privacy

Chess Tournament Manager is a general-audience tournament administration tool for clubs, schools, academies, and arbiters, and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Game Wings Support does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.

Although tournament organizers choose what player names and ratings they record locally on their own devices, the app does use Google AdMob and Google UMP over the internet when online. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has submitted personal information directly to us (such as via support email), please contact gamewingssupport@gmail.com so we can promptly delete it.

8. International Data Processing & Policy Updates

  • International Processing: While your local SQLite tournament data remains on your device, third-party ad requests and consent checks handled by Google AdMob / UMP may be processed on Google's servers in regions outside your home country in accordance with Google's privacy frameworks.
  • Updates to This Privacy Policy: We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. Changes become effective when posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Previously, our September 27, 2026 revision documented Version 1.0.1 AdMob and UMP integration; this November 26, 2026 update reconciles telemetry wording, removes the inaccurate in-app consent-button statement, adds explicit local data deletion steps, and aligns disclosures with Version 1.1.3.
