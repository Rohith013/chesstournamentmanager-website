Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

At Game Wings Support, we prioritize the privacy and security of your data. The Chess Tournament Manager application is designed with a strong focus on offline functionality and local data ownership.

1. Data Collection & Storage

  • No User Account Required: You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or log in to use the application.
  • Local Storage: All tournament data, player profiles, pairings, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • No Cloud Synchronization: In Version 1.0.1, there is no automatic cloud synchronization or backup to remote servers. Your data stays entirely on your device.
  • Exported Files: Any PDF reports or backup files you generate remain strictly under your control. You choose when and how to share them.

2. Analytics and Tracking

  • Log Data and Analytics: Whenever you use our Service, in a case of an error in the app, we collect data and information (through third-party products) on your phone called Log Data. This Log Data may include information such as your device Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing our Service, the time and date of your use of the Service, and other statistics.

3. Advertisements

  • Third-Party Services and Advertising: Our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Specifically, we use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies and device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to serve ads based on your past visits to this app or other websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

    Link to the privacy policy of third-party service providers used by the app:
    • Google AdMob Privacy Policy

4. GDPR & CCPA Consent

  • European Economic Area (EEA) and UK Users (GDPR): If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK, we use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather your consent before displaying personalized advertisements. You have the right to withdraw your consent or change your ad preferences at any time within the app settings or via your device's ad settings.
  • California Residents (CCPA): We do not "sell" your personal information in the traditional sense. However, the use of third-party advertising identifiers may be considered a "sale" under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You can opt out of interest-based advertising by navigating to your device settings (e.g., "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).

5. App Permissions

The application may request the following permissions on your device solely for core functionality:

  • Storage/Files: Required only to save PDF reports and backup files to your device's local storage.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect user data or transmit information over the internet, it is safe for users of all ages.
