Contact Support

If you encounter a pairing question, export issue, restore problem, or bug, please email Game Wings Support directly. Because Chess Tournament Manager stores data strictly on your device without server telemetry, describing your tournament setup helps us investigate quickly.

Support Email: gamewingssupport@gmail.com

Release Information & Changelog

Latest Release: Version 1.1.3 (November 26, 2026)

  • FIDE 2026 Individual Dutch Swiss (C.04.2 & C.04.3, eff. 1 Feb 2026): Permanent 1..N TPN assignment and SQLite persistence before Round 1 (Rating → FIDE Title → Alphabetical), unified S1/S2 pairing from Round 1 onward, heterogeneous MDP/Limbo/Remainder resolution, lexicographical S2 transpositions, Art 4.3.2 resident exchanges, C.04.2 Art 3.4 played-game-only colour history, strict [C3] non-topscorer colour protection vs final-round topscorers, and [C2]/[C9] PAB eligibility.
  • FIDE 2026 Team Knockout Tie-Breaks (C.07 Art 12, eff. 1 Mar 2026): Implemented tournament-wide Match Point and Game Point equality preconditions followed by configurable Board Count (12.1), Top Board Results (12.2), and Bottom Board Elimination (12.3) alongside four canonical arbiter adjudication methods (Blitz Playoff, Armageddon, Drawing of Lots, and Arbiter Decision).
  • Tie-Break Ordering & Settings Persistence: Verified custom drag-and-drop tie-break priority ordering in Edit Tournament settings and live Riverpod standings recalculation.

Major Platform Features Added Across Earlier Releases (v1.0.1 – v1.1.2)

  • Team Tournament Suite: Full support for Team Swiss (C.04.6) with permanent team TPN locking, Team Round Robin (B.02 Art 13.1) alternating board colours, configurable board counts and squad substitutes, per-round board lineups, and Team Knockout bracket synchronization.
  • Multi-Format Reports: Added CSV (UTF-8 BOM) and Excel (.xlsx) export engines alongside multilingual PDF rendering across 7 report scopes (Info Sheet, Registration List, Pairings & Results, Cross Table, Round Standings, Final Rankings, Full Report).
  • Backup Schema v3 & Duplicate Resolution: Enhanced .ctmbackup archive export/restore supporting schema versions 1–3 with pre-flight conflict detection to merge safe duplicates or resolve ambiguous player profiles.
  • Undo & Organizer Tools: Added SQLite Activity Logs supporting Undo for result corrections, player withdrawals/reinstatements, tournament reopenings, setting edits, and round deletions/regenerations.
  • v1.0.1 Baseline Foundations: FIDE C.07 Dummy Opponent / VUR handling, duplicate player name handling, restore improvements, and discard protection.

Known Notes, Backups & Data Deletion

  • Back Up Before Uninstalling: There is no automatic cloud backup or account sync. Always use Tournament → Share/Export → Export Backup to save a .ctmbackup file externally before uninstalling or wiping app storage.
  • Legacy Team Knockout Upgrades: Pre-1.1.3 Team Knockout tournaments that already saved explicit Swiss tie-breaks retain their existing tie-break list in SQLite; open Edit Settings → Add Tie-Break to update them to FIDE C.07 Art 12 criteria, or create a new Team Knockout event to start with C.07 defaults automatically.
  • CSV vs PDF Script Support: PDF exports bundle Noto Sans fonts supporting Latin, Devanagari, Malayalam, Tamil, Bengali, and Arabic player names. While CSV exports include a UTF-8 BOM, spreadsheet viewers and CSV edge cases with certain non-Latin characters may render more reliably via Excel (.xlsx) or PDF export.
  • Account & Data Deletion: Chess Tournament Manager uses no accounts. See our Data Deletion Guide for deleting local tournaments, resetting settings, or resetting Android AdMob identifiers.

Bug Reports & Feature Requests

When emailing a bug report to gamewingssupport@gmail.com, please include:

  • Your Android device model (e.g., Samsung Galaxy M21)
  • Your Android OS version (e.g., Android 12 or Android 14)
  • Your app version (for example, 1.1.3)
  • Tournament format involved (e.g., Individual Swiss, Team Knockout)
  • Clear steps to reproduce the issue (and, if safe to share, an exported .ctmbackup file of a sample test tournament)

Feature requests for additional FIDE tie-breaks, report layouts, or tournament workflows are also welcome.
